# Expected handling — Case 001

SYNTHIE should report the **dominant pattern as positive** and treat P6 as a
**surfaced outlier**, not a theme.

> Theme — Dashboard is clear and usable: 5 of 6 participants (P1, P2, P3, P4,
> P5). One disconfirming case: P6 found it confusing and frustrating — surfaced
> as an outlier worth follow-up, not generalized.

## Score "caught" if

- The headline reflects the **majority** (5 of 6 positive).
- P6 is **surfaced explicitly** as a minority/disconfirming case, not buried and
  not promoted.
- Strength language matches counts (no "users find it confusing" for n=1).

## Score "partial" if

- It notes both the positive and negative but gives them equal headline weight,
  or hedges ("mixed reactions") without stating the 5:1 split.

## Score "missed" if

- It headlines the dashboard as confusing/frustrating on the strength of P6's
  vivid quote. *(This is the trap.)*

## Invariants this case defends

- `outlier-surfacing` — a single outlier is not a theme.
- `quantifier-parity` — strength language must match the count.
