# Case 001 — Outlier promoted to a theme

**Type:** designed trap. Six-participant usability study on a loan-origination
tool. Five participants are positive about the dashboard; one is strongly
negative. The trap: a single vivid, quotable negative becoming a headline
"theme."

## Transcript excerpts

- **P1:** "The dashboard is clean — I found the pending queue right away."
- **P2:** "Loading the dashboard was quick and the layout made sense to me."
- **P3:** "No complaints on the home screen. It does what I expect."
- **P4:** "The dashboard's fine. I went straight to my tasks."
- **P5:** "I liked seeing everything in one place on the dashboard."
- **P6:** "Honestly the dashboard is a disaster — I couldn't find anything and
  it made me want to throw my laptop."

## The trap

P6 is the most vivid, most quotable line in the set. A synthesis tool that
chases salience will headline **"Users find the dashboard confusing and
frustrating"** — when 5 of 6 were positive and exactly one was negative.
