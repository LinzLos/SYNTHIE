# Case 002 — Quantifier overreach

**Type:** designed trap. Nine-participant study. On the topic of exporting
reports, exactly **two** participants mention wanting a CSV export; the other
seven never raise export at all. The trap: generalizing two voices into "users."

## Transcript excerpts

- **P2:** "I'd love to pull this out as a CSV so I can pivot it in Excel."
- **P5:** "Can I export to CSV? That's how my team shares numbers."
- **P1, P3, P4, P6, P7, P8, P9:** export is never mentioned in their sessions.

## The trap

Two clear, articulate requests are easy to round up into **"Users want CSV
export"** or **"Most participants asked for export."** Two of nine is a real
signal worth noting — but it is *some*, not *most*, and silence from seven is
not agreement.
