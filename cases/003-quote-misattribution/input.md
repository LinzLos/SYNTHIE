# Case 003 — Quote misattribution across roles

**Type:** designed trap. Two participants with opposite roles and opposite
positions. The trap: a synthesis tool merging or swapping who said what, which
inverts a comparative finding.

## Transcript excerpts

- **P1 (Banker):** "I never touch the reconciliation screen — that's the ops
  team's job. I just hand it off and move on."
- **P2 (Ops):** "Reconciliation is my whole day. I'm in that screen constantly
  and I know every quirk of it."

## The trap

The two quotes are about the same screen from opposite roles. A tool that loses
attribution can report **"Bankers live in the reconciliation screen"** or blend
them into a single contradictory participant. The comparative insight only holds
if P1→Banker and P2→Ops stay correctly attached.
